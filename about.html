About Us 关于我们

Learn about our history, mission, and the people who make it all possible 了解我们的历史、使命和让这一切成为可能的人们

Our Mission 我们的使命

To serve our communities with compassion, empower youth through meaningful programs, and create lasting positive change through volunteer action and community engagement.

以关怀服务社区，通过有意义的项目赋能青年，并通过志愿行动和社区参与创造持久的积极变化。

Our Vision 我们的愿景

A Canada where every community thrives, every young person has opportunities to succeed, and kindness and service are at the heart of society.

一个每个社区都蓬勃发展、每个年轻人都有成功机会、善良和服务成为社会核心的加拿大。

Our Values 我们的价值观

Compassion, Integrity, Excellence, Inclusivity, and Community. These core values guide everything we do and every decision we make.

同情心、诚信、卓越、包容性和社区精神。这些核心价值观指导着我们所做的一切和我们做出的每一个决定。

About Lions Clubs International 关于国际狮子会

Founded 1917 成立于1917年

Lions Clubs International was founded by Melvin Jones in Chicago, Illinois, USA. What started as a vision to unite service clubs has grown into the world's largest service organization, dedicated to serving communities and meeting humanitarian needs.

国际狮子会由梅尔文·琼斯于美国伊利诺伊州芝加哥创立。最初只是一个联合服务性社团的愿景，现已发展成为世界上最大的服务组织，致力于服务社区和满足人道主义需求。

Our Motto 我们的座右铭

"We Serve"

This simple yet powerful motto encapsulates the essence of Lions: service to others. Lions are known for their commitment to improving communities through volunteer service and humanitarian work.

这个简单而有力的座右铭概括了狮子会的精髓：为他人服务。狮子会以通过志愿服务和人道主义工作改善社区的承诺而闻名。
