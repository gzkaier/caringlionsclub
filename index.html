Who We Are 关于我们

Dedicated to making a positive impact in our communities 致力于为社区带来积极影响

York Region Caring Lions Club is a non-profit organization committed to community service, youth development, and volunteer empowerment. We believe in the power of collective action to create lasting positive change in our communities.

加拿大关爱狮子会是一家非营利组织，致力于社区服务、青年发展和志愿者赋能。 我们相信集体行动的力量，能够为社区创造持久的积极变化。

Proud Member of Lions Clubs International
Serving communities worldwide since 1917 | 1.4M+ members in 200+ countries

国际狮子会自豪成员
自1917年以来服务全球社区 | 200多个国家140万会员
